01

A quiet room to come back to.

Ritual Studio is built around a simple habit: an hour of slow, deliberate work on the reformer or the mat, often enough that it becomes part of your week.

Your instructor watches how you move, sets the springs for your body and adjusts each exercise so you can feel it working, whether you are new to pilates or have practised for years.

02

  • Reformer

    Springs, straps and a sliding carriage add resistance and support, so every movement is controlled from start to finish.

  • Mat

    The classical pilates sequence on the floor: core strength, breath and posture, using your own body weight.

  • Private sessions

    One to one on the reformer, for a first introduction, an injury you are working around or a specific goal.
