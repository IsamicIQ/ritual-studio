01

A quiet room to come back to.

Ritual Studio is built around a simple habit: an hour of slow, deliberate work on the reformer or the mat, often enough that it becomes part of your week.

Your instructor watches how you move, sets the springs for your body and adjusts each exercise so you can feel it working, whether you are new to pilates or have practised for years.

02

Your first visit

  1. Book your spotPick a class on the timetable at least one day ahead (same-day bookings aren't available) and choose how you want to pay.
  2. Arrive ten minutes earlyTime to settle in and tell your instructor about any injuries before class starts.
  3. Bring grip socks and waterGrip socks keep you steady on the reformer. Wear clothes you can stretch in.
