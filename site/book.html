Every class is 50 minutes. Read about each class below, then pick a day and a time on the timetable and pay by M-Pesa, card or at the studio.

Book at least one day ahead. Same-day bookings aren't available, so book by the day before your class.

  • Ritual Signature

    A full-body burn in our 50-minute dynamic reformer session. It combines a classical and dynamic repertoire for a fun, balanced workout, designed to build a strong, functional and flexible body.

  • Ritual 101 · Intro

    Perfect for anyone new to the reformer. Over 50 minutes we guide you through introductory moves, with slightly slower transitions and a stronger focus on the fundamental principles of the Pilates method. Start with the intro: 2 classes at a discounted price.

  • Ritual Power

    Get ready for lift-off. We add a jump-board to the reformer for the ultimate high-energy class, challenging your cardio endurance and toning you from head to toe with fun, creative flows.

    Not suitable for pre- or post-natal clients, or for beginners.

  • Hot Pilates

    Six classes a week, from Monday to Saturday. New to it? The intro gives you 3 sessions within a week.

  • Cold Plunge

    On its own or after class: Fire and Ice pairs it with Hot Pilates, and Pilates and Plunge with a reformer class.
